Home insurance FAQ

What does homeowners insurance cover?

Typically your dwelling, other structures, personal belongings, loss of use and personal liability. Wind and hurricane coverage often comes with a separate deductible in Florida.

Does my policy cover flood?

No. Flood is excluded from standard homeowners policies and needs its own coverage. We can quote that too.

Does roof age matter?

Yes. Many Florida carriers look closely at roof age and condition. Tell us about yours up front so we can match you with the right carrier.

My mortgage lender needs proof of insurance. Can you help?

Yes. We provide evidence of insurance to your lender and can update it if your mortgage company changes.
