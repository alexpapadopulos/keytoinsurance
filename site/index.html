FAQ

Quick answers.

How fast can I get a quote?

Usually the same day, often while you’re on the phone. Call 786.417.2459 and have your driver’s license, vehicle details or property address handy.

Do you work with more than one insurance company?

Yes. As an independent agency we shop multiple carriers, including Progressive, Geico, Universal and Citizens, so you aren’t locked into one company’s price.

Can you help if I’ve had accidents or tickets?

Often, yes. Our carrier mix includes options for a range of driving records. Call us and we’ll tell you honestly what’s available.

What does homeowners insurance cost in Florida?

It varies a lot by roof age, construction, location and coverage. We’ll quote it side by side across carriers so you can see the real tradeoffs.

Do I need flood insurance?

Standard homeowners policies don’t cover flood damage. In South Florida it’s worth a serious look whether or not your lender requires it. See our flood coverage page.

What if I need to file a claim?

Call us first. We’ll help you start the claim, explain what to expect, and follow up so it doesn’t fall through the cracks.
