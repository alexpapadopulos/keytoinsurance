Flood Insurance

Homeowners policies don’t cover flood. In South Florida, a few inches of water can mean tens of thousands in damage.

Ask about flood coverage
  • Covers damage from rising water, storm surge and heavy rain
  • Often required by lenders in flood zones, and smart outside them too
  • Coverage for the structure and, optionally, your belongings
  • Typically has a waiting period, so don’t wait for a storm warning

Renters Insurance

Your landlord’s policy covers the building, not your stuff. Renters insurance is inexpensive and protects what you own.

Ask about renters coverage
  • Personal property protection for theft, fire and covered damage
  • Liability coverage if a guest is injured or you damage someone else’s property
  • Additional living expenses if you’re displaced
  • Often required by apartment leases

Business Insurance
